Profit & Loss

Profit = SP - CP | Loss = CP - SP | % = (Profit or Loss ÷ CP) × 100

Discount & Markup

Discount = (MRP × Rate)/100 | Markup = CP × (1 + %/100)

Simple & Compound Interest

SI = (P×R×T)/100 | CI = P(1+R/100)^T - P

Loan EMI

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] ÷ [(1+R)^N – 1]

Currency Conversion

Converted = Amount × Rate

Break-even Analysis

BEP = Fixed Cost ÷ (SP – VC)

Tax Calculations

Tax = Amount × (Rate/100)

ROI (Return on Investment)

ROI % = (Gain – Cost) ÷ Cost × 100
